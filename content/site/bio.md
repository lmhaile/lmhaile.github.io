I am a **Research Assistant and PhD student** within the Department of Infectious Disease Epidemiology at Imperial College London. My interests lie in the use of modelling methods to estimate the epidemiologic impact of vaccines for polio and malaria. I also retain an interest in the epidemiology of chronic diseases, particularly for chronic respiratory disorders, musculoskeletal disorders, and hearing loss.  

<br>
<br>
Prior to this role I was a **Technical Analyst** at Imperial, where I applied context-specific malaria modelling to estimate the long-term vaccine impact of RTS,S and R21/Matrix-M, the two currently available pre-erythrocytic malaria vaccines. I estimated vaccine impact across Sub-Saharan Africa for the Vaccine Impact Modelling Consortium, a project funded by the Wellcome Trust to produce estimates of vaccine impact across the Gavi portfolio.

<br>
<br>
Previously I modelled the global burden of hearing loss, musculoskeletal disorders, headaches, hearing loss, and chronic respiratory conditions for the Global Burden of Disease study at the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation. I have a background in Public Health **(Brown)** and Health Metrics and Evaluation **(University of Washington, MPH)**.
